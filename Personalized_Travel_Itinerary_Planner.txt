Personalized Travel Itinerary Planner

The Personalized Travel Itinerary Planner is a full-stack, AI-powered web application that simplifies travel planning through intelligent automation and personalized recommendations. Built with a React frontend, FastAPI backend, and Supabase integration, it generates customized day-by-day itineraries based on user preferences, budget, and travel style using prompt engineering and generative AI. The system delivers optimized plans that include attractions, dining, accommodations, and transportation in a clear and structured format.

With a modern SaaS-style dashboard, smart trip builder, and interactive itinerary view, the platform reduces decision fatigue and saves planning time. Users can quickly generate, save, edit, and regenerate trips while receiving curated recommendations tailored to their needs. By automating research and logistics, the application enables travelers to focus more on experiences rather than planning complexity.

Scenario 1: Solo Traveler Planning a Weekend Getaway

A solo traveler wants to plan a short weekend trip but has limited time to research attractions and routes. They enter their destination, budget, travel dates, and interests such as food and sightseeing into the planner. Within seconds, the system generates a balanced itinerary that optimizes travel time, suggests nearby attractions, and recommends affordable dining spots. The traveler follows a clear, structured plan and avoids spending hours searching across multiple websites, resulting in a smooth and stress-free getaway.

Scenario 2: Couple Planning a Personalized Vacation

A couple planning a romantic vacation wants a customized experience rather than generic tourist recommendations. They select preferences like relaxed pacing, scenic spots, and premium stays. The platform uses AI to create a day-wise itinerary including curated restaurants, unique experiences, and optimized travel routes. They can modify activities, regenerate specific days, and track estimated costs, allowing them to collaboratively refine their plan and enjoy a tailored trip that aligns with their expectations.

Scenario 3: Small Group Coordinating a Multi-Day Trip

A group of friends organizing a multi-day holiday faces challenges coordinating schedules, budgets, and shared interests. Using the planner’s dashboard, they create a shared trip, input group size, and combine preferences such as adventure, culture, and budget limits. The system automatically structures activities, allocates time efficiently, and provides logistics suggestions to minimize conflicts and travel delays. This centralized planning approach reduces coordination overhead and ensures everyone enjoys a well-organized and memorable group travel experience.
